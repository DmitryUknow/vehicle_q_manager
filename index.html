Contract Plant Quality Manager

Warranty-driven quality management for SKD vehicle assembly

OEM representative → Factory quality control → Field defect elimination

Key activities

    Required outputs

    DetectMarket or plant signal
    ProtectContain affected vehicles
    ExplainFind occurrence and escape causes
    CorrectImplement permanent actions
    ProveVerify effectiveness in plant and field

    Daily operational control

    • Review new warranty claims, critical customer complaints, DTC logs, photos and returned-part requests.
    • Join the factory quality meeting and define priorities, owners and due dates.
    • Walk the assembly line and observe critical operations, torque control, connector installation and adjustment processes.
    • Inspect blocked vehicles, work-in-process, reworked units and suspect component batches.
    • Review end-of-line, PDI and road-test defects and compare them with field complaints.
    • Follow up open 8D actions, containment checks and overdue investigations.
    • Escalate safety, regulatory, stop-shipment and high-cost risks.

    End-of-day status

    • New and repeated defects
    • Blocked VINs and component lots
    • Containment results
    • Action owners and deadlines
    • Escalations and decisions required

    Weekly management cycle

    • Build a Pareto analysis by symptom, system, component, supplier, production date and repair cost.
    • Review warranty trends and claims per 1,000 vehicles.
    • Hold cross-functional problem-solving reviews with the plant, engineering, suppliers and warranty teams.
    • Audit critical or recently changed processes and verify implementation on the shop floor.
    • Review returned-part analysis, NTF cases and supplier 8D reports.
    • Update the risk list, corrective-action tracker, clean points and affected VIN ranges.
    • Report progress, overdue actions and required management decisions.

    Weekly deliverables

    • Warranty Pareto and trend report
    • Open issue and 8D tracker
    • Audit findings
    • Action effectiveness status
    • Management escalation list

    Event-driven activities

    • Support new-model launches, trial builds and the first mass-production batches.
    • Approve or update inspection procedures, control plans and PDI checklists after a new defect.
    • Conduct special audits after process, software, component or supplier changes.
    • Organise stop-shipment, vehicle inspection, sorting or rework for critical issues.
    • Support service campaigns or recalls: affected VIN definition, repair procedure, parts and cost estimation.
    • Visit suppliers, dealers or technical centres for joint investigations.
    • Transfer lessons learned to other models, processes and future launches.

    Decision triggers

    • Safety or legal risk
    • Rapidly increasing claim rate
    • Repeated defect after closure
    • Major process change
    • New model or new supplier

    Vehicle and process checks

    • Visual, functional, mechanical and diagnostic inspections.
    • Body gaps, paint, corrosion, damage and water-leak checks.
    • Steering, suspension, braking, wheel alignment, noise and vibration checks.
    • Infotainment, heating, electrical and ADAS function checks.
    • Tool calibration, traceability, operator instructions and Poka-Yoke verification.

    Non-conforming product control

    • Quarantine and identify suspect vehicles and components.
    • Define inspection, sorting and rework instructions.
    • Verify repaired vehicles before release.
    • Maintain VIN, component lot and machine-kit traceability.
    • Confirm the clean point before normal shipment resumes.
